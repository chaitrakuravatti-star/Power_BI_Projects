<H1>HR Analytics Dashboard – Power BI</H1>

Project Overview

This Power BI project focuses on Human Resource Analytics, providing deep insights into employee attrition, workforce demographics, salary distribution, tenure patterns, and job role performance.
The dashboard helps HR teams and leadership identify attrition drivers, monitor workforce health, and make data-driven retention decisions.

Objectives

•	Analyze overall employee attrition and workforce size

•	Identify attrition trends by age, education, salary, tenure, and job role

•	Compare attrition across departments

•	Understand salary bands contributing to high attrition

•	Support HR decision-making with interactive insights


Key Insights

•	Early-career employees are more likely to leave

•	Lower salary bands contribute the highest attrition

•	Sales and technical roles show higher attrition compared to others

•	Education background impacts employee retention

•	Gender-based attrition varies significantly by salary range


Tools & Technologies Used

•	Power BI Desktop

•	DAX (Attrition Rate, KPIs, calculated measures)

•	Power Query (data cleaning & transformation)

•	CSV / Excel (data source)


Data Modeling

•	Single fact table with calculated dimensions

•	Key DAX Measures:

    o	Attrition Count
    
    o	Attrition Rate (%)
    
    o	Average Salary
    
    o	Average Age

o	Average Years at Company

•	Optimized model for slicer performance



Filters & Interactivity

•	Department selection buttons

•	Dynamic visuals reacting to user input

•	Drill-down enabled for age, salary, and tenure analysis



Business Use Case

•	Improve employee retention strategies

•	Identify high-risk attrition segments

•	Optimize salary and promotion policies

•	Support HR planning and workforce optimization
