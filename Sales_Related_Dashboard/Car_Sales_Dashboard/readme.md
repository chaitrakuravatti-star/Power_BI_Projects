<h1>Car Sales Dashboard</h1>

Project Overview

The Car Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze and visualize automobile sales performance across multiple dimensions such as time, geography, vehicle attributes, and manufacturers.
It delivers a centralized analytical view for tracking sales trends, identifying top-performing segments, and understanding customer preferences through an intuitive, dark-themed interface.

This project demonstrates end-to-end Power BI skills including data modeling, DAX calculations, time intelligence, and interactive reporting.

_______________________________________________________________

Business Objectives

•	Track Year-to-Date (YTD) and Month-to-Date (MTD) sales performance

•	Monitor average selling price trends over time

•	Identify top-performing car companies, dealers, and regions

•	Analyze customer demand by body style, color, engine, and transmission

•	Enable stakeholders to make data-driven sales and inventory decisions

____________________________________________________________________

**Dashboard Features**

**Executive KPI Cards**

    YTD Total Sales
    
    YTD Average Price
    
    YTD Cars Sold
    
    MTD metrics with YoY growth indicators

**Trend Analysis**
    
    Weekly sales trend visualization
    
    Peak and low sales identification

**Categorical Analysis**

    Sales by Body Style
    
    Sales by Vehicle Color

**Geographical Analysis**

    Dealer-wise sales distribution using map visuals

**Company Performance**

    Company-wise comparison of sales, cars sold, and average price

**Detailed Transaction View**

    Drill-through table with car, customer, dealer, and sales details

**Sales Performance Analysis**

    Decomposition Tree to identify key drivers of YoY sales growth

**Tools & Technologies Used**

    Power BI Desktop – Report development and visualization
    
    Power Query Editor – Data cleaning and transformation
    
    DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – KPIs, YoY, YTD, MTD calculations
    
    Microsoft Bing Maps – Geo-spatial sales visualization
    
    Star Schema Data Modeling – Optimized performance and scalability
