<h1>E-Commerce Sales Dashboard</h1>

**Project Overview**

The E-Commerce Sales Dashboard is an interactive Power BI report designed to analyze online sales performance across categories, products, regions, customer segments, and shipment types.
The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales, profit, quantity, and profit margin trends, enabling stakeholders to quickly assess business performance and identify growth opportunities.

This project showcases data modeling, DAX measures, time-based analysis, and interactive visual storytelling using Power BI.

_________________________________________________________________________________-

**Business Objectives**

    Track Year-to-Date (YTD) sales, profit, quantity, and profit margin
    
    Analyze sales performance across product categories and products
    
    Identify top and bottom performing products
    
    Evaluate sales distribution by shipment type and customer segment
    
    Understand regional sales contribution across the United States
    
    Support strategic decisions related to pricing, logistics, and product focus

  ____________________________________________________________________________

  **Dashboard Features**
🔹 **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**
      
    YTD Sales
    
    YTD Profit
    
    YTD Quantity
    
    YTD Profit Margin
    
    Year-over-Year (YoY) growth indicators with trend visuals
    
    These KPIs provide an instant snapshot of overall business health.
    
🔹** Category Performance Analysis**
    
    Sales comparison by Office Supplies, Furniture, and Technology
    
    YTD vs PYTD sales comparison
    
    YoY percentage growth with trend indicators

🔹 **Product-Level Insights**

    Top 5 Products by Sales
    
    Bottom 5 Products by Sales
    
    Helps identify high-revenue drivers and underperforming items

🔹** Shipment Type Analysis**

    Sales distribution by shipment modes:
    
    First Class
    
    Same Day
    
    Second Class
    
    Standard Class
    
    Highlights logistics performance and cost-efficiency opportunities

🔹 **Geographic Analysis**

    Sales by State using map visualization
    
    Regional performance tracking across:
    
    West
    
    East
    
    Central
    
    South

🔹** Customer Region Analysis**

    Sales contribution by customer region
    
    Percentage share visualization for regional comparison

**Tools & Technologies Used**

    Power BI Desktop – Dashboard development and visualization
    
    Power Query Editor – Data cleaning and transformation
    
    DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – KPI calculations and time intelligence
    
    Microsoft Bing Maps – Geographical sales visualization
    
    CSV / Excel Dataset – Source data

** High-Level Insights**

    Standard Class shipping contributes the highest share of total sales
    
    Office Supplies consistently generate stable revenue
    
    A small number of products drive a significant portion of total sales
    
    Regional sales performance varies, with certain regions outperforming others
    
    Profit margin trends highlight opportunities for pricing and cost optimization
    
    Customer segment behavior differs across regions and shipment types
