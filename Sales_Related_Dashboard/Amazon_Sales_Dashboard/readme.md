<h1> Sales Analysis | Amazon Products </h1>

**Project Overview**

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive view of Amazon product sales performance. It is designed to help stakeholders understand overall sales trends, product category performance, customer engagement through reviews, and time-based sales patterns. The report supports data-driven decision-making by offering interactive visuals and intuitive navigation.
_______________________________

**Business Objective**

The primary objective of this dashboard is to:

•	Monitor overall sales performance across products and categories

•	Compare sales trends across different time periods

•	Analyze product-wise contribution to sales and reviews

•	Identify seasonality and peak selling periods

•	Support strategic planning using high-level sales insights
______________________________________________

Dashboard Highlights

The dashboard includes multiple analytical sections that collectively provide a holistic sales overview:

🔹 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
    
    •	Year-to-date sales performance
    
    •	Quarter-to-date sales trends
    
    •	Product sales volume overview
    
    •	Customer review activity summary

These KPIs give a quick snapshot of business performance at a glance.
____________________________________________

Sales Trend Analysis

  •	Sales by Month: Shows how sales fluctuate over different months, helping identify seasonal patterns and growth trends.
  
  •	Sales by Week of Year: Provides a granular view of weekly sales behavior, useful for understanding short-term trends and demand cycles.
__________________________________________________

Category-Level Performance

  •	Sales by Category: Compares performance across different product categories.
  
  •	Includes year-to-date, previous period comparison, and growth trend indicators.
  
  •	Helps identify high-performing and underperforming categories.
__________________________________________

Customer Behavior Analysis

  •	Sales by Weekend vs Weekday: Highlights differences in customer purchasing behavior based on day type.
  
  •	Product Reviews Analysis: Shows customer engagement and feedback volume by product descriptions.
__________________________________________

Product-Level Insights
  
  •	YTD Sales by Product Description: Identifies products contributing most to overall sales.
  
  •	YTD Reviews by Product Description: Highlights products receiving higher customer attention and engagement.

________________________________________________________________

Filters & Interactivity

The dashboard is fully interactive and allows users to slice and dice data using:

•	Product Category filter

•	Year filter

•	Quarter filter

All visuals respond dynamically to filter selections, enabling focused and customized analysis.
