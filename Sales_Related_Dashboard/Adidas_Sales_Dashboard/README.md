<h1> Adidas Sales Analysis</h1>

**Project Overview**

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive view of Adidas sales performance across time, geography, sales channels, products, and retailers. The dashboard is designed to help business users understand overall sales trends, regional contributions, and key drivers that influence sales outcomes, without focusing on specific numeric values.
The report supports both high-level monitoring and detailed exploration through interactive visuals and filters.
_________________________________________

Objectives

    •	Analyze overall sales performance and profitability trends
    
    •	Understand sales distribution across regions, states, and cities
    
    •	Compare performance by sales method and retailer
    
    •	Identify key factors that influence increases in total sales
    
    •	Enable interactive exploration for business and strategic insights

______________________________________________

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

The top-level KPI cards provide a quick snapshot of overall business performance, including:

    •	Overall sales performance
    
    •	Overall profitability
    
    •	Total units sold
    
    •	Average selling price per unit
    
    •	Average profit margin

These KPIs help users quickly assess business health before exploring deeper insights.

_______________________________________________________

Dashboard Pages & Visuals

1. Sales Overview

This section focuses on high-level sales performance and trends:

•	Sales Trend by Month: Visualizes how sales change over time, highlighting seasonal patterns and demand fluctuations.

•	Sales by State (Map View): Displays geographic distribution of sales across states, enabling regional comparison.

•	Sales by Region: Shows contribution of each region to total sales using comparative visuals.

________________________________________

2. Regional & Retail Analysis

This section provides insights into where and through whom sales are generated:

•	Sales by Region (Bar Chart): Compares regional performance side by side.

•	Sales by Retailer: Highlights the relative contribution of major retailers to overall sales.

•	Region Toggle (Retail / Region View): Allows users to switch perspectives between regional and retail-focused analysis.

________________________________________

3. Sales Contribution by Geography

This drill-down view enables hierarchical exploration of sales:

•	Region → State → City Breakdown: Shows how sales flow from broader regions down to individual cities.

•	Helps identify high-performing and emerging geographic markets.

•	Supports interactive selection to focus on specific regions or states.
_________________________________________________

Key Influencers Analysis

The Key Influencers visual identifies factors that contribute to increases in total sales:

•	Examines the impact of sales method

•	Highlights the role of geographic regions

•	Identifies influential product categories

This feature helps stakeholders understand why sales change, not just where or when.
___________________________________________________

Filters & Slicers

The dashboard includes interactive slicers that allow users to customize their analysis:

•	Sales Method: Filter results based on the type of sales channel

•	Invoice Date Range: Analyze performance over a selected time period

•	Region, State, and City: Focus on specific geographic areas

These filters update all visuals dynamically for seamless exploration.
________________________________________

User Interaction Features

•	Cross-filtering between visuals for deeper analysis

•	Drill-down and drill-through capabilities in geographic views

•	Toggle buttons for switching analytical perspectives

•	Hover tooltips for contextual information

________________________________________

Tools & Technologies Used

•	Power BI Desktop for data modeling and visualization

•	DAX for calculated measures and business logic

•	Power Query for data transformation and cleaning
