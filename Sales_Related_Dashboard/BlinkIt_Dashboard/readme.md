<h1>Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard</h1>

Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Sales Analysis Dashboard for Blinkit (India’s Last Minute App) built using Power BI. 
The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance, customer behavior, outlet characteristics, and product-level insights. It is designed to support data-driven decision-making for business stakeholders by transforming raw transactional data into meaningful visual insights.
________________________________________________________________

**Business Objectives**

•	Monitor overall sales performance across outlets and product categories

•	Analyze customer preferences and purchasing patterns

•	Evaluate outlet performance based on size, type, and location tier

•	Understand the impact of product attributes such as fat content and item type

•	Support strategic planning for inventory, pricing, and outlet expansion

______________________________________________________________________


**Dashboard Features**

The dashboard is structured into multiple analytical sections:

🔹 **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)**

    Overall sales performance
    
    Average sales per transaction
    
    Number of items sold
    
    Average customer rating

These KPIs provide a quick snapshot of business health at a glance.

🔹 **Sales Trend Analysis**

    Outlet establishment trend over time to understand growth patterns
    
    Sales distribution across different time periods

🔹 **Product Analysis**

Sales contribution by item type

    Comparison based on product attributes (e.g., fat content)
    
    Identification of high-performing and low-performing product categories

🔹 **Outlet Analysis**

    Performance comparison by outlet size (small, medium, high)
    
    Sales analysis by outlet location tiers
    
    Outlet type comparison to evaluate operational efficiency

🔹 **Customer Insights**

    Analysis of customer ratings

Relationship between ratings, sales, and item visibility

____________________________________________________

**Interactivity & Filters**

    The dashboard includes multiple slicers and interactive elements:
    
    Outlet Location
    
    Item Type
    
    Metric selector (Sales, Average Sales, Number of Items, Ratings)

These filters allow users to drill down into specific segments and perform focused analysis dynamically.

**Tools & Technologies Used**

    Power BI Desktop – Data modeling and dashboard development
    
    Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
    
    DAX – Calculated measures and KPIs
    
    Microsoft Excel / CSV – Source data format

**High-Level Insights:**
    
    Sales performance varies significantly across outlet sizes and location tiers
    
    Certain product categories contribute more consistently to overall sales
    
    Outlet type and visibility play a key role in driving performance
    
    Customer ratings show a relationship with sales and repeat purchases
