**Project Overview**

This Power BI project provides an end-to-end analysis of Uber trip data, focusing on booking trends, revenue insights, trip distances, time-based patterns, vehicle performance, and location analysis.
The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders understand customer behavior, demand patterns, and operational performance across different time periods and cities.

**Objectives**

1.	Analyze overall Uber trip performance

2.	Track total bookings, revenue, and trip distances

3.	Identify peak booking times and high-demand days

4.	Compare vehicle types based on usage and revenue

5.	Understand pickup & drop-off location trends

6.	Enable interactive filtering by date and city

**Key Insights
**
•	UberX is the most preferred vehicle type across locations

•	Majority of bookings occur during day trips

•	Peak booking hours are observed during midday to evening

•	Uber Pay is the dominant payment method

•	Certain locations consistently generate higher demand

•	Longer trips contribute significantly to total revenue

•	DAX (for calculated measures & KPIs)

•	Calculated measures for:

o	Total Bookings

o	Total Booking Value

o	Average Booking Value

o	Total & Average Trip Distance

**Filters & Interactivity
**

•	Pickup Date range slicer
