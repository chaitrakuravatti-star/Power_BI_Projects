<h1> Credit Card Analysis Report – Power BI Dashboard </h1>

📌 Project Overview

This Power BI project presents an end-to-end analysis of credit card transactions and customer behavior. The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders understand transaction patterns, customer demographics, revenue distribution, and usage trends across multiple dimensions. It provides an interactive and visually intuitive way to explore credit card performance and customer profiles.
________________________________________

Objective

The primary objectives of this dashboard are:

    •	To analyze overall credit card transaction activity
    
    •	To understand revenue contribution across different customer segments
    
    •	To identify spending patterns based on card type, usage method, and customer demographics
    
    •	To provide actionable insights for business and marketing decision-making

The dashboard is divided into two main sections:

1️⃣ Credit Card Transaction Report

    Focuses on transaction-level performance and spending behavior.

2️⃣ Credit Card Customer Report

    Focuses on customer demographics, income patterns, and behavioral segmentation.

**Credit Card Transaction Report – Description**

🔹 Key Metrics Section

Provides a high-level summary of transaction performance, including:

•	Overall revenue generated

•	Total transaction amount

•	Interest earned from transactions

•	Total transaction volume

These metrics help users quickly assess overall business performance.
________________________________________
🔹 **Quarterly Performance Analysis**
      
      •	Displays transaction trends across different quarters
     
      •	Compares revenue with transaction volume over time
      
      •	Helps identify seasonal or periodic patterns in credit card usage
________________________________________
🔹 **Revenue Breakdown Analysis**
    Revenue is analyzed across multiple dimensions, including:
        
        •	Card Category (e.g., different tiers of credit cards)
        
        •	Expense Type (such as daily expenses, lifestyle spending, and essentials)
        
        •	Education Level of customers
        
        •	Job Type and employment category
            
        •	Card Usage Method (swipe, chip, online)
    
    This allows a deeper understanding of where and how customers are spending.
________________________________________
🔹 **Interactive Filters**
**Users can dynamically filter the report using:**
    
    •	Time-based slicers
    
    •	Card category selections
    
    •	Usage method filters
    
    •	Customer segmentation options
    
These filters enhance exploration and personalized analysis.

**Credit Card Customer Report – Description**

🔹 **Customer Overview Section**

Provides a summarized view of customer-related performance, including:
   
    •	Revenue contribution by customers
    
    •	Income distribution
    
    •	Interest generation

________________________________________
🔹 **Demographic Analysis**

    •	Age Group
    
    •	Marital Status
    
    •	Education Level
    
    •	Dependent Count
This helps identify key customer segments and behavioral trends.
________________________________________
🔹 **Geographic Analysis**
    
    •	Shows revenue contribution across different states or regions

__________________________________________

Customers are segmented based on:

This analysis supports targeted marketing and product strategy decisions.
