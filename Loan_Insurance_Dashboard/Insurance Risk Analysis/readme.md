<h1> Insurance Risk Analysis – Power BI Dashboard</h1>

** Project Overview**
The Insurance Risk Analysis dashboard is a Power BI report designed to analyze insurance policy distribution and risk-related attributes across multiple customer, vehicle, and coverage dimensions. The dashboard provides a holistic view of policy characteristics to support risk assessment, underwriting analysis, and strategic decision-making.
________________________________________

**Objective**

    The primary objectives of this dashboard are:
    
    •	To analyze insurance policy distribution across different customer segments
    
    •	To understand policy patterns based on vehicle usage and characteristics
    
    •	To assess coverage distribution across geographic zones
    
    •	To support risk evaluation and portfolio analysis
    
    •	To enable interactive and exploratory insurance data analysis

________________________________________

**Key Policy Summary**

This section provides a high-level overview of insurance policy metrics, including:

    •	Total insurance policies
    
    •	Overall claim-related indicators
    
    •	Claim frequency overview
    
    •	Average claim-related measures

It offers a quick snapshot of the insurance portfolio.
________________________________________

**Policy Analysis by Vehicle Usage**

This section analyzes insurance policies based on:

•	Commercial vehicle usage

•	Private vehicle usage

It helps compare risk exposure across different types of vehicle usage.

**Coverage Zone Analysis**

This section visualizes policy distribution across:
  
    •	Urban coverage zones
    
    •	Rural coverage zones

It supports geographic risk profiling and regional policy assessment.

**Policy Distribution by Vehicle Make**
