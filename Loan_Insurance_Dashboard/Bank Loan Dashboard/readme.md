<h1>Bank Loan Report – Power BI Dashboard</h1>

<b>Project Overview</b>

This Bank Loan Report Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of loan applications, funding, repayments, and borrower characteristics. The dashboard helps banking and financial stakeholders monitor loan performance, identify risk patterns, and track key lending KPIs across time, geography, loan purpose, and customer attributes.
The report is structured into three main pages: Summary, Overview, and Details, enabling both high-level monitoring and detailed record-level analysis.

Objectives

•	Analyze overall loan application and funding performance

•	Track Month-to-Date (MTD) and Month-over-Month (MoM) trends

•	Compare good vs bad loan distribution

•	Identify loan performance by state, purpose, term, and employment length

•	Monitor interest rate and DTI (Debt-to-Income) patterns

•	Enable drill-down into individual loan records

•	To analyze repayment behavior and loan status trends


Dashboard Coverage & Key Analysis Areas

1️⃣ Loan Portfolio Overview

Provides a high level summary of the bank’s loan portfolio, giving stakeholders a quick understanding of overall loan activity and performance.

2️⃣ Loan Status Analysis

Analyzes loans based on their current status, helping identify healthy loans, potential risks, and areas requiring attention.

3️⃣ Customer Demographics Analysis

Explores loan distribution across customer attributes such as:

•	Employment details

•	Income groups

•	Home ownership categories This helps understand which customer segments are most engaged in borrowing.

4️⃣ Loan Purpose & Category Analysis

Breaks down loans by purpose and category to identify major borrowing trends and understand customer financial needs.

5️⃣ Repayment & Credit Behavior

Analyzes repayment patterns and credit related factors to assess borrower behavior and potential default risks.

6️⃣ Geographic Analysis

Shows loan distribution across regions or locations, enabling geographic level insights into lending activity.

7️⃣ Time Based Trends

Tracks loan trends over time to observe changes in lending behavior and portfolio growth or contraction patterns.
